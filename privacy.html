Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information I collect through this website, why I collect it, who I share it with, how long I keep it and what your rights are. I've written it in plain English. If anything isn't clear, please get in touch.

What I collect

When you ask for a free preview using the form on this website, I collect:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your phone number
  • your current website address, if you give it

If you email or phone me, I'll also have your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to tell me.

I don't ask for, and please don't send, sensitive information such as health details or bank details through the form.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like every website, it has to send your device's IP address (its internet address) to the services that deliver the page: GitHub, which hosts the site, and Google Fonts, which supplies the lettering. They use it to send you the page and to keep their services secure.

Why I use your information

I use the details you send me to:

  • contact you about your free preview
  • prepare the preview and answer your questions
  • agree and carry out the work, if you decide to go ahead

The law says I need a reason, called a "lawful basis", to use your information. Mine are:

  • Steps before a contract: you've asked me for a preview and a quote, so I need your details to respond (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Legitimate interests: following up your enquiry in a reasonable way, keeping basic records of who I've spoken to, and keeping this website secure (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

I won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing unless you've said yes to that separately. I don't sell your information, and I don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Who I share it with

I only share your information with the services I need to run this website and my business. They act on my instructions and must keep it safe:

  • Formspree, which receives the form and passes it to my email. Formspree's privacy policy.
  • My email provider, which stores the messages I receive.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • My accountant and professional advisers, only if you become a client and it's needed for my business records.

I may also share information if the law requires it, for example with HM Revenue and Customs or the police.

Information sent outside the UK

Formspree and GitHub are based in the United States, so your information may be stored or processed there. When that happens, it's protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the international data transfer agreements approved by the UK Information Commissioner. You can ask me for more detail.

How long I keep it

  • If you don't go ahead: I delete your enquiry 12 months after we were last in touch.
  • If you become a client: I keep your details for as long as I'm working for you, then for 6 years after our work ends, because UK tax and company law requires businesses to keep their records for that long.

How I keep it safe

This website is served only over a secure (HTTPS) connection. My accounts are protected with strong, unique passwords and 2-step sign-in, and I only keep the information I need.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
  • ask me to correct anything that's wrong
  • ask me to delete your information
  • ask me to limit how I use it
  • object to me using it, including for any marketing
  • ask me to send it to you or someone else in a common format

To use any of these rights, email or phone me using the details above. It's free, and I'll reply within one month.

How to complain

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first by email or phone, so I can put it right. I'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and give you a full answer as quickly as I can.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
